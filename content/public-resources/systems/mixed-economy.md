## 1. Status / fairness disclaimer

This dossier is a comparative instrument for Constitutional Capitalism. Categories overlap; real societies are usually hybrids. Pejorative or diagnostic labels (where used) describe patterns of power, not identities people must proudly claim.

**Phase 3 status:** Treated as the hybrid baseline in the shared-dial crosswalk. Live panels show Arkansas already mixes public and private across measured domains.

## 2. Definition

A mixed economy combines private markets with substantial public ownership, regulation, and social provision—the empirical default of modern capitalism rather than a pure ideology.

**Plain version:** Mixed economy is analyzed here as a political-economic pattern with distinctive ownership, market, and authority arrangements—not as a single timeless nation.

## 3. How it functions

Private firms produce most tradables; governments run or heavily regulate utilities, education, health finance, and transfers. Shares differ widely.

| Dimension | Summary |
|---|---|
| Ownership | Private enterprise dominant with public ownership in selected sectors |
| Property regime | Strong but bounded by regulation and public domains |
| Markets | Markets allocate most goods; planning or regulation in others |
| Government / polity | Regulator, insurer, sometimes producer; shares vary by country |
| Labor / work | Wage labor with public labor law overlays |
| Power & concentration | Competition law, regulation, and occasional public options |
| Accountability | Democratic politics plus market discipline |

## 4. Why it emerges / what sustains it

Political bargaining after industrialization, wars, and depressions; recognition that pure poles are unstable.

## 5. What informed adherents or beneficiaries claim

Pragmatists claim flexibility and the ability to correct market and state failures sector by sector.

These are **reported claims**, not project endorsements.

**Strengths often cited:** Pragmatic hybridity; Sector-specific tools; Empirical commonality.

## 6. Major weaknesses and failure modes

As a category it explains little without specifying which mix. Can hide privilege and blur responsibility.

**Risks tagged in the matrix:** Conceptual vagueness; Inconsistent accountability; Fiscal and regulatory overload.
