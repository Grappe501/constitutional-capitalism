## Purpose

Evidence spine for presumption of openness, legitimate exceptions, the People's Ledger, open procurement/budgets, equal access, and AI-as-interpreter — **before** detailed open-records legislation.

## Rules

- Do not invent FOIA statistics, portal coverage rates, terminal counts, or dollar-trail completeness.
- Separate design agendas (People's Ledger) from operating systems.
- Prefer primary sources (statutes, agency reports, state auditors, court opinions, official transparency portals).
- Preserve contrary evidence (privacy harms, security risks, scraping abuse, cost of unified systems, exemption overbreadth).
- Legal review remains 0% until real legal review occurs.
