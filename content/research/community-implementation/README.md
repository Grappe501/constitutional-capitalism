## Purpose

Evidence and product-design spine for adaptive playbooks, asset-first community blueprints, and a Community Operating System — **before** claiming an operating town-plan product.

## Rules

- Do not invent population trajectories, job counts, or community dashboard values.
- Treat “500 → 5,000 in 30 years” as a design aspiration / evaluation horizon, not a forecast.
- Community OS and Build Your Community tools are design agendas until secure product modules exist.
- AI may organize best practices; it must not make policy.
- Prefer primary community-development evaluations, case studies with real numbers, and honest failure reporting.
- Apply the Implementation Completeness Rule proportionally — exploratory research may mark missing fields rather than invent precision.
