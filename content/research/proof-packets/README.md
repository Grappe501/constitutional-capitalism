# Proof Packets

**Governance:** `data/project/proof_burden_registry.json` · `CC-DEC-094`  
**Related:** Architecture Incubator (`CC-DEC-093`), Diagnosis Brief Standard, forensic audit

## Purpose

Proof Packets turn Architecture Candidates into scholarship tracks. Each packet answers one research front for a principle’s Proof Burden.

## Rules

- Do not invent citations, statistics, multipliers, or modeled results.
- Separate what data establishes, what may be inferred, and what CC normatively concludes (three-layer rule).
- Scaffold files are **not** completed dossiers.
- Architectural surfaces are not evidentiary completion.
- Link registered `CC-SRC-*` / `CC-CLAIM-*` IDs when evidence exists.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `scaffold_only` | Outline exists; research not assembled |
| `in_progress` | Sources being registered and briefed |
| `partial` | Some claims supported; gaps remain |
| `ready_for_three_layer_review` | Packet complete enough for historical / economic / legal reviews |
| `graduated` | Reviews passed; may strengthen publishable doctrine posture |
