**Domain status:** Phase 2 architecture — full research domain

**Last updated:** 2026-08-04

## Purpose

Establish the evidence spine for justice under Constitutional Capitalism **before** detailed justice policy is written.

Justice is not optional ornamentation. It determines whether liberty, ownership, opportunity, and accountability are real.

## Rules

- Do not invent citations or statistics.
- Separate facts, interpretations, normative doctrine, and policy proposals.
- Preserve contrary evidence (public-safety tradeoffs, victim interests, incapacitation effects).
- Prefer primary/official sources (BJS, DOJ OJP, Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts, GAO, state court/corrections statistical reports, peer-reviewed syntheses).
- Link claims to `data/research/claim_ledger.json` and sources to `data/research/source_registry.json`.
- Legal conclusions require later legal review — do not fake it.
