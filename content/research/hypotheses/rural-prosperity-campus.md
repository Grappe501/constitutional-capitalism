# HYP-123 — Rural Prosperity Campus Model

**Status:** Major research hypothesis — **not doctrine** · **not a pilot** · **not a new principle**  
**Update:** UPD-064 · Decision anchor: CC-DEC-102  
**Linked case:** CC-CASE-ROSEBUD-001 · Related network hyp: HYP-124  

## Central hypothesis (testable)

> Can a rural public education system become the coordinating institution for rebuilding a regional food economy while simultaneously improving education, workforce development, family farm profitability, and community prosperity?

Not: “We should build agricultural schools.”

## Defining research questions

1. Can education itself become **productive economic infrastructure**?
2. Can a rural school district function as the coordinating institution for regional economic development **without compromising its primary educational mission**?

Verdicts: **Supports** · **Qualifies** · **Contradicts**.

## What the model describes (for research — unproven)

A **regional rural prosperity campus**: the high school as anchor institution within an interconnected ecosystem.

| Layer | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Education | Core academics + ag/trades/entrepreneurship taught inside functioning businesses |
| 2 Family Farm Network | Partner existing farms as teaching/research/apprenticeship/innovation/demonstration sites — strengthen, don’t replace |
| 3 Natural Resource Partnerships | Conservation/soil/water/grazing/forestry/habitat tools where legally appropriate — no presumed funding |
| 4 Food System | Full value chain: production → consumer |
| 5 Regional Business Cluster | Feed, equipment, vets, processors, logistics, tech, finance, institutional buyers |
| 6 Cooperative Development | Ownership pathways, not only employment |
| 7 Food Security | Local supply priorities + sustainability of any external premium markets — carefully modeled |
| 8 Student Enterprises | Experiential learning across business functions |
| 9 Graduate Transition | Graduation as beginning of mentorship/ownership pathways |

## Empirical questions the stream must answer

- Educational outcomes?
- Family farm profitability?
- Workforce readiness?
- Entrepreneurship?
- Local food security?
- Attraction of new families?
- Financial sustainability?
- Effects on neighboring communities (strengthen vs weaken)?
- Success/failure conditions?

## Comparative placement (UPD-065)

Part of a family of development-engine laboratories. Not “every community should become Rose Bud.” Authenticity of the local prosperity engine comes first; institutional design follows.
