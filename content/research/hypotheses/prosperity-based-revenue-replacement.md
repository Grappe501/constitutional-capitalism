## Objective (locked)

Move revenue away from taxing ordinary work and homeownership toward **use, impact, depletion, visitor, luxury, human-capital, value capture, enterprise, prosperity-fund, and external income**. If compulsory taxation remains necessary after those engines are exhausted, place the residual burden on **substantial accumulated surplus wealth** — **never wages, salaries, or ordinary earned income**.

## Residual Wealth Contribution (active residual instrument)

Last activated. Net-worth floor. Self-retiring. Never ordinary labor income.

**Hierarchy:**  
`Prosperity engines → public returns → external income → residual wealth contribution → never ordinary labor income`

**Formula each year:**  
`obligations − prosperity engines − Prosperity Fund distributions = Residual Wealth Contribution Requirement`
