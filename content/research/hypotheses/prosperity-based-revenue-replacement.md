## Central question

> Exactly what revenue must be replaced, who currently depends on it, and what replacement sources can grow with prosperity without simply shifting the same burden onto ordinary people through another tax?

## Hard distinction

| Target | Ledger | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| Individual income tax | **State Prosperity Revenue Ledger** | Primarily state general revenue |
| Property tax | **Community Prosperity Revenue Ledger** | Local infrastructure finance (schools, counties, cities, fire/EMS, libraries, roads, debt, pensions) |

These are **not** the same reform. Property tax itself is several fiscal problems: school URT, additional school millage, county, municipal, special districts, and debt-service millage.

## First T1–T5 pass (Resource · Enterprise · Consumption)

| Family | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Resource** | Cannot carry meaningful IIT share at bound magnitudes (~$10.6M severance) |
| **Enterprise** | Partial at best (~15% of IIT scale) — and **already spent** in GR |
| **Consumption** | Only family with IIT-scale yield — but existing stream; hike-as-swap is a **regressive household burden rename** unless protections are proven |

**Not counted as replacement dollars:** prosperity-fund dividends, public investment returns, unmodeled community enterprise distributions.

## Replacement-capacity gap (not a political conclusion)

Under honest **incremental** accounting, modeled replacement capacity is **$0**. Full IIT target (~$3.859B) and full community property gap remain. Existing corporate/gaming/severance dollars already fund GR; summing them does not close the gap.

**No recommendation to abolish IIT or property tax.**

## Tax retirement is earned

1. Build **incremental** replacement revenue  
2. Dedicate dollars to a **Tax Retirement Fund**  
3. When recurring replacement meets a safety threshold → automatically reduce the targeted tax  
4. Measure → repeat until zero  

Forbid: abolish billions today → hope prosperity fills the gap.

## Five tests for every replacement source

Revenue capacity · Volatility · Incidence · Prosperity alignment · Behavioral distortion
