## Central question

> Exactly what revenue must be replaced, who currently depends on it, and what replacement sources can grow with prosperity without simply shifting the same burden onto ordinary people through another tax?

## Hard distinction

| Target | Ledger | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| Individual income tax | **State Prosperity Revenue Ledger** | Primarily state general revenue |
| Property tax | **Community Prosperity Revenue Ledger** | Local infrastructure finance (schools, counties, cities, fire/EMS, libraries, roads, debt, pensions) |

These are **not** the same reform.

## Anchors (cited — primary bind required)

- Arkansas **individual income tax FY2026 ~$3.859B** vs **state sales/use ~$3.666B** ([Arkansas DFA](https://www.dfa.arkansas.gov/news/state-completes-fiscal-year-with-655-million-surplus/))
- School **25-mill Uniform Rate of Tax** is constitutional; **2024–25 URT local foundation funding ~$1.603B** estimate ([Arkansas General Assembly funding overview](https://webftp.blr.arkansas.gov/Home/FTPDocument?path=Assembly%2F%2FMeeting+Attachments%2F410%2F27809%2FEXHIBIT+E2+-+Funding+Overview+Report.pdf))
- Property tax funds local services broadly ([UAEX](https://www.uaex.uada.edu/business-communities/local-government/property-taxes.aspx))

## Philosophical goals (research posture)

- State: earn more from system prosperity than from taxing productive labor of individuals.
- Community: own enough productive capacity that growth helps finance the community.
- Every candidate needs **incidence analysis** — sales-tax-only swap is a regressivity risk, not a default.

## Tax retirement is earned

1. Build replacement revenue  
2. Dedicate dollars to a **Tax Retirement Fund**  
3. When recurring replacement meets a safety threshold → automatically reduce the targeted tax  
4. Measure → repeat until zero  

Forbid: abolish billions today → hope prosperity fills the gap.

## Local-up constitutional sequence (sketch)

- **Years 0–4:** pilots + statutory reductions  
- **Years 4–8:** multi-community community-prosperity revenues + equalization  
- **Years 8–12:** redesign school/local funding on demonstrated replacements  
- **Years 12–16:** constitutional amendment only after carry capacity proved  

## Five tests for every replacement source

Revenue capacity · Volatility · Incidence · Prosperity alignment · Behavioral distortion

## Next research slice (not choosing the tax)

Populate the **Arkansas Revenue Replacement Ledger** row-by-row, then study **Resource + Enterprise + Consumption** candidates against the five tests — without treating unmodeled prosperity-fund returns as replacement dollars.
