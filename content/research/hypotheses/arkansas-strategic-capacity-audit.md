# CC-HYP-ARKANSAS-STRATEGIC-CAPACITY (HYP-130)

**Status:** Research hypothesis — **not doctrine** · **not a completed audit**  
**Update:** UPD-127 · Decisions: CC-DEC-112 (`KEEP_AS_HYPOTHESIS`), deepened by CC-DEC-114  
**Demands:** `data/project/CC_ARKANSAS_STRATEGIC_CAPACITY_AUDIT_DATA_DEMANDS.json`

## Central question

> What critical professions, industries, and technical capacities does Arkansas need but not currently educate enough people for?

## Capability map chain

Build from the bottom up:

1. **what Arkansas has**  
2. **what Arkansas needs**  
3. **what Arkansas imports**  
4. **what Arkansas lacks**  
5. **what capacity could realistically be built**

## Sector coverage target

Education · healthcare · agriculture · energy · manufacturing · technology · infrastructure · skilled trades · public services · other strategically important capabilities.

## Map layers

1. **Current capacity** — universities, community colleges, trade schools, apprenticeships, extension  
2. **Needs** — critical professions/industries/technical capacities for local prosperity and reduced dependency  
3. **Imports** — out-of-state training, residencies, firms, credential pipelines  
4. **Missing capacity** — where students or institutions must leave Arkansas  
5. **Buildable capacity** — what could realistically be built under existing or near-term authority (feeds HYP-129)

Example domains (research list, not findings): rural medicine, maternal care, dentistry, veterinary medicine, AI systems, semiconductor manufacturing, advanced agriculture, nuclear engineering, energy systems, water infrastructure, industrial automation.

## Holds

- No invented enrollment or graduate statistics  
- Shortage dials ≠ proof a program is absent  
- Does not displace baseline definition-locks
