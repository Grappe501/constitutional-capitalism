## Purpose

Evidence spine for banking, insurance, healthcare, pharmaceuticals, community health, and preventive design — framed as **system design and incentives**, not inherent corruption — **before** detailed sector legislation.

## Rules

- Do not invent banking, insurance, healthcare, pharmaceutical, or Community Health Index statistics.
- Do not assert unsupported systemic-intent claims (e.g., that pharmaceuticals are designed to keep people sick).
- Prefer researchable questions about incentives, competition, transparency, access, and outcomes.
- Separate design agendas (Community Health Index) from operating programs.
- Prefer primary sources (FDIC, Fed, NCUA, NAIC/state insurance, CDC/NCHS, CMS, NIH/GAO pharma research literature).
- Preserve contrary evidence (legitimate finance functions; prevention cost-effectiveness variation; innovation incentive tradeoffs).
