## Purpose

Evidence spine for human capital infrastructure, equal-dignity pathways, and regional academy pilots — **before** statewide academy legislation.

## Rules

- Do not invent enrollment, retention, earnings, or business-start statistics.
- Treat regional academies as a **pilot design agenda**, not a locked statewide system.
- ASMSA may be cited as an institutional reference for residential public academies; do not treat it as a sourced outcome evaluation until registered evidence exists.
- Prefer primary sources (NCES, BLS, state education/workforce agencies, apprenticeship data).
- Preserve contrary evidence (selection effects, cost, mobility limits of regional specialization).
