## Central question

How should a society design essential systems so that private innovation and investment are rewarded while the public interest remains protected?

## Why it matters

Banking, insurance, healthcare, and pharmaceuticals belong in Constitutional Capitalism. Framing them as system-design and incentive problems — rather than as inherently corrupt — makes the philosophy more rigorous and persuasive. Related foundational briefs remain at `10-healthcare-and-economic-security.md` and `13-banking-and-capital.md`.
