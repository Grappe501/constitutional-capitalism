## Central question

How might economic concentration relate to democratic accountability — and what is established vs contested?

## Why it matters

The project’s civilizational frame asks where power should reside; diagnosis must not leap from market shares to capture.

## Current findings (sourced inventory)

Economic concentration is measurable (`CC-SRC-005`; wealth concentration `CC-SRC-001` / `CC-SRC-002` / `CC-SRC-012`). The causal claim that concentration **weakens democratic accountability** (`CC-CLAIM-003`) remains **requires_additional_research**: campaign finance, lobbying, media ownership, and revolving-door modules are not completed in this slice.

Honest Phase 2 posture: treat concentration as a **risk factor and research priority**, preserve contrary institutional checks (elections, courts, media pluralism where present), and refuse partisan diagnosis.

- **Established from registry:** Contemporary wealth and industry concentration snapshots (`CC-SRC-001`, `CC-SRC-002`, `CC-SRC-005`, `CC-SRC-012`). SCF 2019–2022 narrowing episode qualifies any “ever-rising” story (`CC-SRC-001`).

### Economic evidence

- **MISSING EVIDENCE:** Lobbying spend, campaign-finance, and media-ownership series registered and linked to `CC-CLAIM-003`.

### Constitutional / legal evidence

- **MISSING EVIDENCE:** No registered campaign-finance cases, First Amendment holdings, or disclosure statutes in this brief’s source IDs. Legal review remains **0%** project-wide. Do not invent constitutional conclusions from CR tables.

## What the data directly establishes

1. Household wealth is highly concentrated on official Fed products (`CC-SRC-001`, `CC-SRC-002`, `CC-SRC-012`).
2. Industry concentration ratios are measurable via Economic Census products (`CC-SRC-005`).
3. These concentration facts do **not** by themselves establish democratic capture or weakened accountability (`CC-CLAIM-003` opposing_evidence; support_level requires_additional_research).

## What may reasonably be inferred

- Concentration creates a factual backdrop that makes accountability risks worth studying — **inference**, risk factor not proof.
- Countervailing institutions (elections, courts, media pluralism where present) can blunt capture — **plausible contrary institutional reading**.
- Digital media concentration may not map cleanly onto traditional industry CR measures — **open**.

## What Constitutional Capitalism normatively concludes

Republican government presupposes that private economic power remains accountable to the people through law. That is a **foundational normative stake**, not a slogan-as-proof. Diagnosis feeds later design; it does not draft a campaign-finance bill here.
