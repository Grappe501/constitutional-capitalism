## Central question

How should a constitutional republic deliberately build human capital infrastructure so every child has a meaningful pathway to productive adulthood — including through pilotable regional models that make home stronger?

## Why it matters

Fits rural communities, productive capacity, geographic opportunity, local leadership, ownership, families, and education connected to community needs. Related foundational brief: `09-education-and-human-development.md`.

## Contrary evidence

- Pathway returns differ and change over time.  
- Residential models can raise access and selection issues.  
- Regional specialization can mismatch student interests without cross-region mobility.  
- Credentials can diverge from actual skill.

## Uncertainties

- Optimal academy naming and governance.  
- Pilot scale and evaluation design.  
- Measurement of “making home stronger” without punishing mobility.
