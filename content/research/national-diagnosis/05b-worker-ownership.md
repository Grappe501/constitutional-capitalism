## Central question

What does research show about employee ownership and firm/worker outcomes?

## Why it matters

Broadened ownership is central to Constitutional Capitalism’s developing doctrine; evidence must stay modest and conditional.

## Current findings (sourced inventory)

Kruse (IZA World of Labor, 2016) synthesizes 100+ studies: employee ownership is generally associated with better average productivity, pay, job stability, and survival, with **small** average performance effects and difficult causal identification (`CC-SRC-006`).

NCEO research digests report similar average associations and some crisis-resilience findings, with advocacy-affiliation caveats (`CC-SRC-007`).

`CC-CLAIM-004` is **partially_supported**: “can improve under some conditions,” not “always improves.” Free-rider problems, selection into ESOPs, complementary HR practices, and retirement-risk concentration are first-class contrary points.

## Evidence tracks

### Historical evidence

- **Established from registry:** Multi-study research program summarized through 2016 (Kruse) and later NCEO digests (`CC-SRC-006`, `CC-SRC-007`).
- **MISSING EVIDENCE:** Registered post-2016 comprehensive meta-analysis update; official long-run ESOP prevalence series attached to baseline.

### Economic evidence

- **Established from registry:** Small average positive performance association (meta-analytic r̄≈0.04 cited in Kruse synthesis) (`CC-SRC-006`); NCEO cites related establishment findings with advocacy caveats (`CC-SRC-007`).
- **MISSING EVIDENCE:** Official aggregate ESOP wealth in SCF-compatible form; cooperative vs ESOP vs broad-based equity comparative modules with registered sources.

### Constitutional / legal evidence

- **MISSING EVIDENCE:** No registered ERISA/ESOP statutory or case-law sources in this brief’s source IDs. Ownership participation relates to economic citizenship as doctrine — not as a sourced legal mandate.

## What the data directly establishes

1. Research syntheses report a **small** average positive association between employee ownership and firm performance, with hard causal identification (`CC-SRC-006`).
2. Free-rider problems and complementary workplace practices are recognized mechanisms affecting outcomes (`CC-SRC-006`).
3. NCEO digests report similar directional associations and must be labeled advocacy-affiliated secondary (`CC-SRC-007`).
4. The data do **not** establish that employee ownership always improves outcomes, nor that a national EO mandate is evidence-required.

## What may reasonably be inferred

- Under some designs (participation, information, complementary HR), EO can improve productivity/retention — **conditional inference** aligned with Qualifies.
- Selection into ESOPs can bias observational associations upward — **plausible**.
- Undiversified employer stock can raise worker financial risk — **recognized contrary mechanism**.

## What Constitutional Capitalism normatively concludes

Broadened ownership is a developing doctrinal priority. Evidence supports **conditional exploration**, not a mandate of a single firm form. Normative economic-citizenship goals must stay labeled as normative.

- Official ESOP wealth / prevalence series for baseline.
- Governance-design mediator studies with clear identification.
- ERISA/ESOP legal source registration.
