## Purpose

Build the evidence spine for distributing political power **before** detailed election, redistricting, or legislative-procedure policy is written.

## Rules

- Do not invent citations or statistics.
- Separate holdings of law, institutional facts, interpretations, and normative doctrine.
- Prefer primary sources (Supreme Court opinions, statutes, state constitutions, FEC, Census, state election/redistricting authorities, NCSL as secondary institutional reference).
- Preserve contrary evidence (party competition benefits, commission capture risks, direct-democracy plutocracy risks, term-limit knowledge-loss effects).
- Legal conclusions require later legal review.
