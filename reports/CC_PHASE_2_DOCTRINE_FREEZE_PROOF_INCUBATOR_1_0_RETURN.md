## Four layers (must not advance at the same speed)

1. Ideas (doctrine architecture)  
2. Evidence (empirical support)  
3. Models (economic / legal / constitutional mechanisms)  
4. Claims (manuscript-ready statements)  

## Exception test (before any new principle)

Must answer at least one:

- Which Phase 2 gate does this close?  
- Which claim does this strengthen?  
- Which evidence dossier requires it?  
- Which constitutional mechanism depends on it?  

If none → Hypothesis Card, not main doctrine.

## Proof Packets

One proposition per packet. Required sections include precise claim, literature, history, counterarguments, limitations, economic evidence, legal considerations, constitutional implications, confidence rating.

**Graduation requires three independent reviews:**

1. Historical evidence  
2. Economic evidence  
3. Constitutional / legal evidence  

Only then may a card graduate into publishable doctrine. Burt step 2 (three-layer retrofit) is the workflow, not a side task.
