## Verdict

Constitutional Capitalism adopts a **constitutionally defensive** national-security doctrine — neither hawkish nor pacifist. Military power serves the Constitution and a free flourishing society; it is not the center of national life.

## Core lines

- **Governing:** Armed Forces defend the nation, deter aggression, honor lawful commitments, and preserve peace through strength — never substituting for constitutional government, diplomacy, or popular sovereignty.
- **Loyalty:** Constitution → People → Republic → Civilian Government → Armed Forces.
- **Deterrence:** Victory measured by wars prevented.
- **Veterans:** Civic leadership pathways; healthcare as service-rendered obligation, not political privilege.
- **National Resilience:** Endure and recover — communities as the foundation of strength.
