## Verdict

Register **HYP-133** as a major transition module: education as **public capital investment in human capability**, not an unlimited transferable benefit and never branded “free.” Prefer a **positive residency-and-service compact** over exit penalties. No new principle. No invented legal clearance.

## Design posture locked

> Arkansas should treat education as a public capital investment in human capability—not as an unlimited transferable benefit with no expectation of contribution back to the state.

Language: publicly guaranteed · publicly financed · resident-invested · prosperity-funded — **not** “free.”
