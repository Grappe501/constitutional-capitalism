# CC-PR-084 — No Knowledge Qualification for Suffrage

**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Class:** Research / design constraint registration (not a new principle)  
**Constraint:** `CC-RDC-NO-KNOWLEDGE-QUALIFICATION-FOR-SUFFRAGE`  
**Hypothesis (separate):** `CC-HYP-AGE-16-LOCAL-SUFFRAGE`

## What changed

We separated **the right to vote** from **civic preparation for voting**.

## Constraint (locked)

> Constitutional Capitalism may advocate universal civic preparation and may investigate age-16 local suffrage, but civic education, testing, certification, academic achievement, service hours, or political knowledge shall not be prerequisites for exercising the franchise.

## Sequencing

1. Build strong **universal** civic education and participation infrastructure.  
2. Separately study extending **local** suffrage to age 16.

## Rejected framing

> Complete our civics program and earn the right to vote at 16.

## Correct framing

> Society has an obligation to prepare every young person for citizenship. Separately, the framework proposes examining whether citizens should begin exercising local suffrage at 16.

## Why this matters for Constitutional Symmetry

If civic training became a voting qualification, whoever controlled curriculum, tests, certification, exemptions, or funding could indirectly control access to the franchise.

## Philosophical line

**Educate everyone; enfranchise by law — not by examination.**

## Hypothesis status

`CC-HYP-AGE-16-LOCAL-SUFFRAGE` remains **NOT ENOUGH EVIDENCE** and must receive full falsification treatment before any Supports / Qualifies / Contradicts verdict.
