# CC Data-Dense Publication Standard 1.0 — Lock Report

**Decision:** `CC-DEC-104`  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Approved by:** Steve (direction locked in conversation; executed as publication governance)

## Why now

The RedDirt → CC public-statistics bridge is live. The project can stop treating statistics as background footnotes and start treating them as the public interrogation layer of the theory.

## What was locked

1. Core publication rule: show reliable data where the theory is encountered.
2. Presentation hierarchy: Idea → evidence → comparison → implication → uncertainty → source.
3. Progressive disclosure (accessible first view; deep Explore layer).
4. Standard Evidence Panel schema for major pages.
5. Contradiction / counterevidence requirement.
6. Honesty labels for missing, stale, national-only, associative, or modeled data.
7. Traceability rule binding displayed stats to canonical assets (bridge-ready).
8. Explicit refusal of cherry-picking, false precision, scoreboard inflation from scaffolding, and new principle IDs.

## What this is not

- Not a new `CC-PRIN-*`
- Not permission to invent numbers
- Not a Phase 8/9 engine build
- Not an immediate redesign of every page

## Immediate product implication

Future page and manuscript work should assume **data-dense by default**. UI Evidence Panels may be scaffolded in a follow-on slice; the rule is binding even before the component exists (manual panels / structured sections are acceptable interim forms).

## Next recommended slice

**`CC-EVIDENCE-PANEL-AND-PUBLIC-SURFACE-BINDING-1.0`**

Scaffold the reusable Evidence Panel on one high-traffic proof/theory surface, bind it to at least one imported RedDirt observation and one existing baseline metric, and prove progressive disclosure without clutter.
