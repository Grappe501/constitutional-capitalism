# CTE Recurring vs One-Time Funding Analysis 1.0

**Slice:** CC-PHASE-2.1-ARKANSAS-CTE-OUTCOMES-FUNDING-AND-RURAL-MAGNET-FEASIBILITY-1.0
**Status:** RESEARCH - no invented budgets

## Rule

Specialty magnets **cannot** count startup grants, one-time equipment awards, or competitive launch funds as sustainable operating revenue.

## Recurring (ops-capable candidates)

| Source | Tag | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation / district CTE teacher formula | RECURRING / FORMULA | Adequacy CTE mix (CC-SRC-139) |
| VCA / Vocational Center Aid | RECURRING / PER-STUDENT-FTE tiered | OSD rules (CC-SRC-140); historical VCAF ~$20.1M (CC-SRC-138) |
| Employer ongoing partnerships | VARIABLE / EMPLOYER | Not guaranteed |

## One-time / time-limited (not sustainable ops)

| Source | Tag | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| State Start-Up Grants | COMPETITIVE / CAPITAL-ISH | **Do not** treat as ongoing ops |
| Perkins V | TIME-LIMITED GRANT / federal formula rules | Useful; not a full ops substitute |
| Capital/facilities | ONE-TIME or debt | Separate from annual instruction ops |

## Unknown structure detail

H2 incentives, concurrent-credit cost share, WIOA/apprenticeship detail, exact current VCA tier dollars - **UNKNOWN** or not harvested as a schedule this slice.

## Magnet implication

A rural specialty magnet that relies on start-up grants without a credible recurring stack (foundation + VCA-like FTE aid + durable local partners) fails the sustainability test even if launch capital is secured.
