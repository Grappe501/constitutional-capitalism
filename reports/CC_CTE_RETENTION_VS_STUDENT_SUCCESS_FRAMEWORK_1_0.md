# CTE Retention vs Student Success Framework 1.0

## CRITICAL

**Student success is not community retention.**

A graduate who completes a credential, earns higher wages, and moves away is an education success and a **rural revitalization failure mode** (train-and-export) unless ownership, employers, housing, and quality of life create credible local futures.

| Metric class | Examples | Revitalization relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Student success | Graduation, credential, wage, placement | Necessary, not sufficient |
| Community retention | Live/work locally N years post-exit | Core falsifier for magnet-as-revitalization |
| Ownership / firm birth | New local businesses, apprentices retained | Ecosystem hypothesis chain |
| Local employer absorption | Hires from center/magnet into regional firms | Demand-side test |

Public evidence harvested this slice: local retention largely **UNTESTED**. Dougherty (CC-SRC-138) speaks to concentrator outcomes vs HS CTE - not community retention.
