## Scope executed

Organized Arkansas transition concepts into a **four-layer research framework** with legal/time horizons, Layer-1 magnet-hub flagship, funding-scan categories, and explicit links to HYP-122–128 / LCL. **Not doctrine**, **not a statewide plan**, **no new panel**, **no grant applications**.

## Central question

> How do we move power, ownership, capability, and decision-making closer to ordinary people without breaking constitutional protections, markets, or civil liberties?

## Layers

1. **0–4y** Immediate programs under existing law — Rural Agriculture Magnet Hub  
2. **4–8y** Community wealth systems — coops, grocery, capital pools  
3. **4–12y** Civic infrastructure between elections — assemblies, participatory budgeting, nonpartisan hubs (HYP-126)  
4. **8–16y** Structural reform research — campaign finance, labor, education, healthcare  

## Alignment holds

- LCL: early work is investigative, not validation theater  
- Expansion: one site → comparable communities (HYP-128) → then scale  
- Layer 4 stays research-bound until evidence + legal review  
- Structural lane unchanged: baseline definition-locks
