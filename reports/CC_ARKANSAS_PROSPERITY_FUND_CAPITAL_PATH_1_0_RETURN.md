## Verdict

The Prosperity Fund can be a **real compounding engine** — and it is **not magic**. At an illustrative 4% sustainable payout, meaningful tax-retirement distributions require enormous permanently invested capital. The fund **does not exist today**, feeder inflows are **illustrative hypotheses**, and **COUNTABLE NOW remains $0**.

## Central question

> How much permanently invested public capital would Arkansas need to generate sustainable annual distributions of $250M, $500M, $1B, and $2B without consuming principal or destabilizing the fund?

### Scale markers (illustrative 4% POMV — not recommendations)

| Sustainable payout | Implied corpus |
|---|---:|
| **$250M**/year | **$6.25B** |
| **$500M**/year | **$12.5B** |
| **$1B**/year | **$25B** |
| **$2B**/year | **$50B** |

Even **$2B**/year is only ~**28%** of the ~$7.23B combined IIT+property map. The Fund is a feeder, not a solo replacement system.

## Hard safeguards (locked)

1. **No tax-retirement credit for unrealized market gains.** Only sustainable distributable cash under the payout rule counts.  
2. **No political raiding of principal.** If this becomes doctrine, principal protection / withdrawal rules should be constitutional-level.  
3. **No raiding existing obligations** (fuel/highway, URT, CIT GR, etc.) merely to make the fund look viable.

## Three growth paths (illustrative feeders)

Year flow modeled:  
`opening → contributions → investment return → inflation reserve → allowable distribution → closing`

Recession stress in years **3** (−15%) and **10** (−12%). Distributions begin only after corpus clears the path’s threshold for a payout target; POMV + inflation-adjusted contributed-capital floor apply.

| Path | Seed | Y1 contrib | Growth | Nom. return | Infl. | POMV | Y16 principal |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Conservative** | $100M | $150M | 2% | 5.5% | 2.5% | 3% | **~$3.9B** |
| **Base** | $250M | $400M | 3% | 7.0% | 2.5% | 4% | **~$11.1B** |
| **Accelerated** | $500M | $1.0B | 5% | 7.5% | 2.5% | 4% | **~$31.9B** |

### 8–16 year window reading

- **$250M:** Plausible inside the window on **Base** (y11) or **Accelerated** (y5) — **if** feeders actually arrive. Conservative misses the window.  
- **$500M:** Accelerated inside (y9); Base just outside (y18); Conservative far outside.  
- **$1B:** Only **Accelerated** inside 16 years (y14) — requires transformational External Income / resource participation.  
- **$2B:** **Not realistic inside 16 years** on these paths (Accelerated y21).

## Feeder buckets

Allowed (when real): lithium/resource participation, windfalls/settlements, public-asset returns, value-capture proceeds, enterprise distributions, external-income surpluses, WPC surplus share **only if** incidence/legal clear.

**Rejected:** raiding existing committed revenues to inflate the corpus.

External Income is the swing variable between Base and Accelerated.

## Three buckets
