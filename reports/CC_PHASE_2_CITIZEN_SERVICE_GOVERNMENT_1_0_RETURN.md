## Verdict

Citizen Public Service is expanded into a full **Citizen-Service Government** model: ordinary working people can serve without sacrificing livelihood, business, career, family security, or retirement — then return to productive community life.

Operational principle: **Serve without sacrifice. Govern without privilege. Return without penalty.**
