# Arkansas Processing — Producer Interview Protocol 1.0

**Slice context:** Introduce **before** drawing intervention conclusions from processor-only testimony.  
**Companion to:** `CC_AR_PROCESSING_HUMAN_VOICE_CALL_PROTOCOL_1_0.md`

## Why both sides

| Side | What it tells us |
| --- | --- |
| Processors | Supply-side constraints, fees, booking, stated bottlenecks |
| Producers | Whether facilities are genuinely usable — distance, wait, cost, trust, economics |

Processor testimony alone cannot certify **economically usable capacity**.

## Identity

Honest research identification (Constitutional Capitalism / Arkansas family-farm processing access). Not a sales pitch. Anonymity available.

## Priority producer geographies (reuse designated set)

Clinton / Van Buren · Rose Bud · Searcy County · Arkansas County · Lafayette County · West Helena / Phillips · Mississippi County — plus producers already using Pottsville / Hope / Atkins paths if identified.

## Core questions

1. Species and approximate annual head marketed  
2. Last place you obtained **inspected** slaughter/processing (if ever)  
3. Last place you used **custom-exempt** (if ever)  
4. How far did you haul? Drive time?  
5. How long did you wait for a date?  
6. What did you pay (if willing)?  
7. Did packaged meat sell? To whom?  
8. After all costs and your time, was it worth it vs selling live?  
9. What failed when it failed? (booking, distance, cost, quality, market, capital, other)  
10. If processing were nearer / cheaper / faster, would you change marketing path?  
11. What do *you* believe is the main barrier?

## Separate two economic questions (for later margin work)

> **Can the farmer make more gross value from the animal?**

> **Can the farmer make more profit after labor, processing, transport, inventory, marketing, spoilage, and working-capital risk?**

Do not collapse these in notes.

## Record file (when interviews begin)

```text
research/phase_2/ar_processing_producer_interview_records.json
```

Create on first completed interview. Until then, absence means **not yet conducted**, not negative evidence.

## Sequencing rule

Do **not** conclude “Arkansas needs more plants” (or any single intervention) from processor calls alone. At least a small producer-side sample should exist before intervention ranking.
