## CC-CLAIM-001

**Current:** Capitalism has produced broad prosperity across historical contexts.

**Why it fails:** Civilizationally sweeping; no registered sources; unfalsifiable as written.

**Defect:** WORDING TOO BROAD

**Removed:** Universal 'capitalism has produced', implied inevitability across all contexts

**Narrowed:** Association not inevitability; requires scoped modules; exceptions explicit

**Evidence:** —

**Confidence:** Very Low until sourced modules exist

**Governance:** REWRITE — then open historical modules before any Supports path.

## CC-CLAIM-010

**Current:** Broader ownership participation would shift many households from wage-only dependence toward multi-source capital ownership.

**Why it fails:** Uses SCF/DFA concentration sources to support a redesign forecast.

**Defect:** FORECAST WITHOUT MODEL

**Proposed:** U.S. household wealth is highly concentrated; whether broader ownership participation would shift households toward multi-source capital income is an untested design prediction requiring modeling and pilots.

**Removed:** Would shift many households; implied empirical forecast

**Narrowed:** Descriptive concentration preserved; prediction labeled untested

**Evidence:** CC-SRC-001, CC-SRC-002, CC-SRC-012

**Confidence:** High for concentration clause; Very Low for prediction clause

**Governance:** REWRITE (split descriptive vs predictive) — or SPLIT into two claims.

## CC-CLAIM-016

**Current:** Online commerce can extract spending from local economies via platform concentration and wealth leakage.

**Why it fails:** Leakage/extraction unproven; scale sources do not establish the mechanism claim.

**Defect:** CAUSAL OVERREACH

**Proposed:** Online commerce is a material share of U.S. retail sales; effects on local spending, platform margins, and community wealth require dedicated leakage/multiplier studies and must not recycle pre-Wayfair tax myths.

**Removed:** can extract; wealth leakage as established mechanism

**Narrowed:** Scale fact; research agenda for leakage; legal update

**Evidence:** CC-SRC-085, CC-SRC-086

**Confidence:** High for retail share; Low for local effects until studies registered

**Governance:** REWRITE — then keep leakage as open research, not claim fact.

## CC-CLAIM-017

**Removed:** threaten ownership security

**Narrowed:** burden/liquidity; relief design; institutional role preserved

**Evidence:** CC-SRC-089

**Confidence:** Moderate

**Governance:** REWRITE
