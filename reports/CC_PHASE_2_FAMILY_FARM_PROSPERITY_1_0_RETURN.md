## Verdict

Local Food and Family Farm Prosperity is registered as a **major dedicated domain** complementing Community Food Security. Framing: family farms as engines of community wealth, public health, land stewardship, and national resilience — profitable regenerative local food systems, not temporary farm subsidies. USDA ERS/FNS/AMS baselines support problem diagnosis. Institutional purchase floors, fund financing, and food-account multipliers are illustrative and require modeling. Legal drafting remains 0%.

**Architecture ≠ Phase 2 proof.** This slice widens the doctrine surface; Burt proof steps remain the forward path.

## Central texts

- **Promise:** A farmer who produces healthy food, restores soil, protects water, employs locals, and feeds the community should earn dependable middle-class-or-better income, build equity, succeed to the next generation, and share in local food prosperity.
- **Farmer message:** Make it profitable to care for the land, feed neighbors, own the farm, and pass something valuable forward — not impose rules without markets and infrastructure.
- **Compact:** Food, land, water, and soil as foundations of national security and community prosperity; rebuild locally rooted family farms with fair markets, regional processing, stewardship pay, ownership pathways, and circulating local wealth.
