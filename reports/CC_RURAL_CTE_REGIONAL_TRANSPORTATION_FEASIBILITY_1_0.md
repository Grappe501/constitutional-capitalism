# Rural CTE Regional Transportation Feasibility 1.0

Regional specialty magnets inherit the same physical constraint as STCs: **time and transport**.

- Sending-district busing to centers: common model; cost bearer and max minutes **UNKNOWN** systematically this slice
- Access map ratings (MODERATE / STRONG / WEAKER) are structural proximity signals - not measured drive-time matrices
- Boarding / residential options: only if evidence supports; not proposed here
- Failure mode: specialty quality exists but rural students cannot reach it daily

No invented route costs.
