## Verdict

Local Ownership Prosperity is registered as a **major dedicated domain**. Framing: reward measurable local prosperity contributions of family, employee, cooperative, and locally controlled enterprises — **not** punish large businesses or prohibit outside investment. Circulation principle is design-testable, not a locked multiplier law. NCEO ESOP and USDA ag-coop baselines support diagnosis. Score weights, capital terms, and reinvestment shares require modeling. Legal drafting remains 0%.

## Central texts

- **Principle:** The closer ownership is to the community, the more likely wealth circulates within that community (test and measure).
- **Objective:** Reward outcomes associated with rooted ownership — not ownership labels alone.
- **Compact:** Communities prosper when ownership is rooted where value is created; expand opportunities without favoring one model by exclusion.
