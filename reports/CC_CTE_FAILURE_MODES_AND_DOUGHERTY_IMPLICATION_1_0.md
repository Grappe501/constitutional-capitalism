# CTE Failure Modes and Dougherty Implication 1.0

## Failure modes

1. **Tracking / second-class pathway** - CTE as dumping ground (CC-SRC-141, CC-SRC-142)
2. **Grant cliffs** - start-up / Perkins treated as permanent ops
3. **Misalignment** - specialty not matched to regional employer demand / ownership pathways
4. **Train-and-export** - student success without community retention
5. **Transport exclusion** - rural distance non-participation
6. **Cream-skimming / sending-district fiscal harm** - UNKNOWN magnitude
7. **Dougherty implication** - centers not clearly better than HS CTE for similar concentrators (CC-SRC-138), so magnets need a **stronger differentiated case** (prestige, academic rigor, employer depth, retention), not "centers exist therefore magnets work"

## What would change our mind

Current center-level outcome tables showing durable wage/placement/retention advantages; recurring funding adequacy; measured access; equity safeguards against tracking.
