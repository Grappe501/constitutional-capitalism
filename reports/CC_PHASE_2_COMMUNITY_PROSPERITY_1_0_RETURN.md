## Verdict

Community Prosperity Accounts are registered as a **major dedicated domain** elaborating Prosperous Aging Layer 3 and linking Civic Wealth contribution pathways. Framing: residents as co-owners of place prosperity through optional local productive investment, Community Investment Units, and Community Equity Credits — **complementing, not replacing**, diversified retirement savings. ICI/MSRB/GAO baselines support diagnosis. Local allocation bands (illustrative 5–20%) and unit instruments require modeling. Legal drafting remains 0%.

**Architecture ≠ Phase 2 proof.** This elaborates an existing aging/civic spine; Burt proof steps remain the forward path.

## Central texts

- **Question:** What if citizens could become long-term investors in the prosperity of the communities they help build?
- **Compact:** Own a stake in the long-term prosperity of the community you help build; raise resident capital for productive public investments; community ownership complements diversified retirement savings.
- **Foundational bridge:** Longer-term investment in local education, energy/infrastructure, and place value so businesses and families stay and arrive.
