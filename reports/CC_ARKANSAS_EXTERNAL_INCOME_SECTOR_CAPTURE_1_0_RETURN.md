## Verdict

Arkansas already pulls material **outside dollars**. That does **not** retire income or property tax. External Income is the critical swing variable because rearranging internal taxes/fees cannot close ~$7.23B. What matters is **Arkansas capture**, not gross export or visitor volume — and only an evidence-backed public-return mechanism can count.

## Central question

> How much money can Arkansas deliberately pull in from outside the state and keep circulating inside Arkansas long enough to create recurring public income, local ownership, and a larger prosperity base?

## Hard wall

> **External income is not automatically public revenue.**

Royalty · lease · participation · enterprise return · workforce contribution · Prosperity Fund feeder — without one of these, dollars stay private circulation even if they enter from outside.

## Capture chain (required for every sector)

`outside dollars entering → Arkansas payroll → Arkansas-owned margin → local supplier spend → state/local public revenue → retained earnings/ownership → leakage back out of state`

### External Income Capture Ratio (modeling tool — not a public baseline)

> What share of outside-origin revenue remains as Arkansas wages, local profits, public revenue, retained ownership, and reinvestment?

A smaller locally owned food-processing ecosystem can beat a much larger externally owned operation on this metric.

## HYP-130 feedback loop

`capacity gap → build program → produce workforce → attract sector → capture external income → grow public wealth → reduce tax burden`

Healthcare, advanced manufacturing, AI/tech, and food-processing skills remain capacity gates. External Income claims that ignore HYP-130 are aspirational.

## Constitutional rejects (unchanged)

- Discriminatory export taxes (food, energy, goods)  
- State import tax for defense  

Preferred: create value **before** goods leave; win federal contracts; retain ownership margins inside Arkansas.

## Implication

This is the first honest framing of the swing variable:

1. Outside demand already exists (tourism proves it).  
2. Leakage and ownership decide whether it becomes Arkansas prosperity.  
3. Public tax retirement still needs **mechanisms**, not press releases.  

**Next:** `CC-ARKANSAS-PUBLIC-INCOME-REPLACEMENT-STACK-1.0` — integrate all 10 engines + **effective** residual wealth base into countable / near-term / 4–16y / stress / optimistic / tax-retirement thresholds.
