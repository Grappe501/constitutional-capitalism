## Verdict

HYP-133’s conceptual spine is hardened around **bona fide residency and Arkansas public investment**, not citizenship. Education converts inherited Arkansas capital into greater human capability; each generation enlarges the inheritance. Nonresident premium access can become **Human-Capital External Income** (HYP-131 join). Public IP claims track **public contribution**, not blanket state ownership of ideas. Four empirical passes remain in order; Demand Map still first when empirical education work begins.

## Frame lock

> Arkansas taxpayers, families, institutions, land, research, infrastructure, and generations of accumulated public investment have built educational assets. Bona fide Arkansas residents should receive the strongest public investment rate; nonresidents should pay a premium that reflects the real value of access to those assets.

**Legal anchor (research posture):** *Vlandis v. Kline*, 412 U.S. 441 — states may use reasonable durational residency requirements to protect preferential higher-education rates for bona fide residents; persons must be allowed to prove bona fide residence; permanent/irrebuttable nonresident labels based solely on prior out-of-state address are out.

## Pricing model (research)

| Band | Rule |
|---|---|
| Pre-K3 → secondary | Publicly guaranteed for bona fide resident children — no tuition concept |
| Resident | Arkansas Resident Investment Rate after defensible domicile test |
| New bona fide resident | Qualifies after legitimate proof — not permanently locked out |
| Nonresident / international | Premium market rate; **optimize** public return, do not blindly maximize price |
| Strategic recruit | Invest when Arkansas wants that person to become an Arkansan (HYP-132) |

## Three legal tests (Pass 2 — separate)

1. Strength of bona fide residency requirements for subsidized higher ed  
2. Lawful and economic nonresident premium  
3. Continuing public return from publicly financed research/assets without harming academic freedom, private innovation, or talent attraction  

## Flow redirection

**Desired:** resources → education → capability → innovation → ownership → wealth → reinvest in Arkansans  
**Rejected:** resources → outside ownership / graduate leaves → wealth exits  

## End state

> Arkansas invests heavily in Arkansans, sells world-class educational capacity to the rest of the world, recruits the people it wants to become Arkansans, and keeps a durable share of the economic value its public institutions help create.
