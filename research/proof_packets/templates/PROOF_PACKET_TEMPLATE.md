## Contrary Evidence

**Mandatory.** Actively seek failures, inefficiencies, distortions, and unintended consequences. Empty contrary sections are a methodology failure unless the search log proves exhaustive negative results.

For every source include the same fields as Supporting Evidence, plus how it challenges the hypothesis.

## Alternative Explanations

Could observed success be explained by geography, population, subsidies, market timing, exceptional leadership, weather, commodity cycles, federal investment, or other unrelated factors? Document each plausible alternative.

---

## Boundary Conditions

Where the doctrine appears strongest. Where it appears weakest. Contexts: rural/urban, income, food deserts, export vs local markets, livestock vs crop, tourism economies, etc.

---

## Failure Conditions

Conditions under which the doctrine is unlikely to work (no processing, no buyers, poor governance, weak demand, capital shortages, etc.).

---

## Implementation Considerations

Workforce, capital, processing, transportation, training, administration, technology, food safety, scaling, and similar practical barriers.

---

## Economic Considerations

---

## Legal Considerations

Research notes only. No legal conclusions. Flag constitutional, commerce, procurement, agency, competition, and cooperative-law open questions.

## Research Gaps
