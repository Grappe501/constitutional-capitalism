# Proof Packet Verdict Standard

**Methodology version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** Every Proof Packet under the Constitutional Capitalism research program.

A verdict is an **evidence judgment**, not a loyalty test. Per CC-DEC-102, every packet must be capable of disproving the doctrine it examines.

---

## Primary investigative verdicts

Use exactly one of these three when closing a research packet on the merits:

### Supports

Registered evidence, after active contrary search, **materially supports** the architectural hypothesis under the stated research question and boundary conditions. Remaining gaps are secondary and do not overturn the core claim.

Supports does **not** mean the full Constitutional Capitalism design is proven ready for law or rollout. It means the investigated proposition is evidence-aligned within documented limits.

### Qualifies

Evidence **partially supports** the hypothesis, or supports it only under narrow conditions, or shows that architecture overclaims relative to what is proven. Qualification language must be specific: what holds, what does not, and what remains unknown.

Qualifies is a successful research outcome. It is the expected result when architecture is ahead of evidence.

### Contradicts

Strongest available evidence **undercuts** the architectural hypothesis as stated, or shows that predicted mechanisms fail under conditions the doctrine assumes. Contradicts triggers architecture review — it is governance success, not project failure.

---

## Lifecycle / process verdicts

These are not substitutes for the three investigative outcomes above when a packet claims research completion on the merits.

### Not Enough Evidence

Temporary research status: searches were performed (or scoped), but the corpus is insufficient to justify Supports, Qualifies, or Contradicts. Packet remains open. Do not use as a soft landing to protect doctrine.

### Withdrawn

Packet abandoned before completion (scope error, duplicate work, or superseded planning). Record reason in registry.

### Superseded

A later packet or methodology revision replaces this packet’s conclusions. Preserve the archive; do not delete the trail.

---

## Rules

1. No compromise wording (“mostly supports,” “leans toward”). Choose one label.
2. Verdict must be justified by cited evidence and contrary treatment.
3. Empty contrary sections without a search log invalidate Supports.
4. Architecture may remain unchanged after Qualifies or Contradicts until a formal doctrine review — but the packet must not pretend the evidence was stronger than it was.
