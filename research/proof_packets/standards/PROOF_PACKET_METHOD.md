# Proof Packet Method Handbook

**Methodology version:** 1.0  
**Decision anchor:** CC-DEC-102  
**Investigator rule:** Discover truth. Do not defend doctrine.

---

## Investigator mindset

You are not counsel for the architecture. You are an investigator assigned to stress-test one proposition.

- Prefer registered sources (`CC-SRC-*`) and claim ledger entries (`CC-CLAIM-*`).
- Do not invent citations, statistics, or case outcomes.
- Label unknowns explicitly.
- Treat “architecture exists” as irrelevant to evidentiary verdict.

---

## Discover truth, not defend doctrine

Success criteria for a packet:

1. Another researcher can reproduce the evidence trail.
2. Contrary evidence was actively sought.
3. The verdict follows from evidence, even if it embarrasses the design.
4. Lessons feed methodology improvement before the next packet.

A packet that returns Qualifies or Contradicts can be a complete success.

---

## Confirmation bias controls

Before writing Supports:

- List the strongest contrary case you can honestly make.
- Ask whether success cases are explained by subsidies, geography, leadership, or federal investment.
- Remove unsupported rhetorical claims from the packet body.
- Separate: (a) what data establishes, (b) what may be inferred, (c) what architecture still only proposes.

---

## Handling contrary evidence

- Search failures: cooperatives, food hubs, procurement systems, processing bottlenecks, cost overruns, governance failures, market distortions.
- Log searches even when results are thin.
- Give contrary sources the same structured fields as supporting sources.
- Do not bury contrary material in footnotes.

---

## Qualifying doctrine

When evidence is partial:

- State what is supported.
- State what is design-only.
- State boundary and failure conditions.
- Recommend future research — not silent architecture expansion.

Qualification does not automatically change principles. Doctrine revision is a separate governance act.

---

## Revision triggers

Recommend architecture or doctrine review when:

- Verdict is Contradicts.
- Verdict is Qualifies and a core mechanism is shown false under assumed conditions.
- New high-quality contrary evidence arrives after Complete status.
- Methodology audit finds systematic bias (then reopen the packet).

---

## Five scholarship questions (every packet)

1. What is the architectural claim?
2. What does the strongest supporting evidence show?
3. What does the strongest contrary evidence show?
4. Under what conditions would this doctrine fail or require qualification?
5. What is the honest verdict: Supports, Qualifies, or Contradicts?

---

## Closure rule

No packet closes without the validation checklist and a methodology audit answering reproducibility and bias questions.
