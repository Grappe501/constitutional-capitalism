## Process questions

1. Did the process follow `PROOF_PACKET_METHOD.md` and the canonical template?
2. Were contrary sources actively sought (with search log if corpus is thin)?
3. Were assumptions identified?
4. Was confirmation bias avoided (architecture defense language removed)?
5. Were unsupported claims removed or labeled as design-only?
6. Can another researcher reproduce this packet from the ledger and citations?
7. Is confidence scored as evidence quality (not certainty)?
8. Is the verdict exactly one allowed label with justification?
9. Were legal notes kept as research questions (no unauthorized legal conclusions)?
10. Were lessons for future packets recorded?

Answer each Yes / No / Partial with evidence pointers (section headings or ledger rows).

---

## Methodology consistency finding

- **Pass** — process consistent; conclusions may still be Qualifies/Contradicts.
- **Pass with recommendations** — process acceptable; improve before next packet.
- **Fail** — reopen packet; do not mark Complete.
