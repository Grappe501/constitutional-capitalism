# Proof Packet Confidence Standard

**Methodology version:** 1.0

Confidence measures **evidence quality** for the judgment being made.  
Confidence is **never** a claim of certainty, prophecy, or political inevitability.

---

## Scale

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Very Low** | Anecdote, single weak source, or large mismatch between claim and evidence. High chance of revision. |
| **Low** | Thin corpus; descriptive baselines only; major gaps in contrary search or comparative design. |
| **Moderate** | Multiple relevant sources of decent quality; key limitations and alternatives documented; still incomplete for strong policy claims. |
| **High** | Robust, convergent evidence across methods/jurisdictions; contrary evidence addressed; residual gaps are bounded. |
| **Very High** | Rare. Near-consensus across high-quality independent lines of evidence with clear boundary conditions. Still not certainty. |

---

## Rules

1. Score components separately (historical, economic, legal, administrative, environmental, community development) before overall.
2. Overall confidence cannot exceed the weakest **material** component for the research question.
3. Official statistics that answer a **narrow descriptive** claim may score High for that claim while overall hypothesis confidence remains Low or Moderate.
4. Legal confidence is research-notes confidence only until formal counsel review exists (currently 0% legal drafting in Phase 2 honesty dials).
5. Do not inflate confidence to protect architecture. Architecture ≠ evidence.

---

## Examples

- USDA descriptive farm-structure statistics for “family farms are most U.S. farms” → may be **High** for that descriptive claim.
- Full Family Farm Prosperity system (guaranteed floors + food utilities + stewardship pay → broader durable prosperity) with only those statistics → overall **Low** or **Very Low**.
