# FDIC QBP community-bank shares

Official **Quarterly Banking Profile Time Series Spreadsheets** extract for publication bind.

- Source page: https://www.fdic.gov/quarterly-banking-profile/quarterly-banking-profile-q1-2026
- Workbook: `qbp-time-series-spreadsheet-first-quarter-2026.xlsx`
- Sheet: `Ratios by CB vs. NCB`
- Bound series: community-bank **share of industry loans & leases** and **share of industry assets** (1984Q1 → latest)
- **Deposit share:** no labeled “Share of Industry Deposits” section in this workbook; registry 13.1% 2024Q4 cell and BankFind CB:1 snapshot retained separately with definition breaks

Regenerate: download the workbook into `.local/downloads/pass9-structural/`, then run the extractor used in Pass 9 structural deepen (see `scripts/pass9_bind_structural_deepen.cjs`).
