# Book Voice and Style Guide

Voice: serious, civic, clear, non-demagogic. Prefer concrete institutions over slogans. Explain before persuading. Respect the reader’s intelligence. Avoid academic fog and partisan pep rallies.

Also follow `docs/writing/ARGUMENT_LAYERS_STANDARD.md`:

- distinguish principles, proposals, predictions, and evidence;
- prefer “proposed constitutional framework intended to strengthen conditions…” over guaranteed national greatness;
- label illustrative ranges as non-modeled illustrations.

## Civilizational framing (proposed)

- Organize around **where power should reside**, not capitalism as an end in itself.
- Prefer “people as the nation’s greatest source of productive capacity…” — never “mere resources.”
- Corporations are **constitutional partners**, not governments and not sole providers of social welfare.
- Acknowledge **subsidiarity** as a shared intuition before extending it.
- Separate **normative principles** from **policy proposals**.
- Invite improvement; do not freeze doctrine.
