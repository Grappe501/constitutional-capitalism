# Constitutional Capitalism Institute (Proposed)

Status: proposed fourth project component  
Decision: `CC-DEC-028`  
Last updated: 2026-08-04

## Why

The project currently has three operating surfaces:

1. The Book (`content/` + manuscript architecture)
2. The Public Website (`apps/book-site`)
3. The Build Board (`apps/build-board`)

A fourth component would reinforce that Constitutional Capitalism is intended as an evolving research-and-evaluation framework, not a static ideology.

## Proposed role

The Institute is not required to be a legal entity on day one. It may begin as a repository domain and public section dedicated to:

- economic modeling;
- constitutional analysis;
- historical comparisons;
- implementation scenarios;
- measurable outcomes;
- policy simulations;
- critiques and responses;
- revisions based on new evidence.

## Suggested repository placement (when authorized)

```text
content/institute/
data/institute/
apps/book-site/src/pages/institute/   # optional public surface
docs/research/                        # shared protocols remain canonical
```

## Relationship to phases

- Phase 2+ research diagnosis feeds Institute registers
- Phase 5 testing/opposition is Institute-intensive
- Phase 9 continuing work assumes Institute-like revision cycles

## Non-goals (near term)

- No authentication/database requirement in early phases
- No claim of academic accreditation
- No invented research findings
- No paid-service dependency

## Immediate action

Record the Institute as an open architectural decision. Do not build a full fourth app until Phase 2/5 readiness and operator approval.
