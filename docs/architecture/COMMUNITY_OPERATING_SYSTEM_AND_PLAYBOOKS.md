## Position in the philosophy

This is where Constitutional Capitalism becomes **practical instead of only theoretical**.

Signature of the movement: **playbooks**.

Practical question:

> Show me exactly how my town of 500 people becomes a town of 5,000 over the next 30 years.

That question is a design aspiration and evaluation horizon — not a forecast or invented case study.

## Permanent rule

> No proposal within Constitutional Capitalism is complete until it includes an implementation pathway, measurable outcomes, estimated costs, anticipated tradeoffs, and a process for evaluation and revision.

## Four levels

1. Philosophy — Why  
2. Constitutional Framework — How society should be organized  
3. Implementation Library — Exactly how  
4. Case Studies — Real communities, numbers, mistakes, successes  

## Seven-layer architecture

Philosophy → Constitution → Research Institute → Implementation Institute → Community Operating System → National Dashboard → Civic Improvement Network

## Community method

Asset inventory first. People first. Local Prosperity Engine: attract → keep → make owners. Incubator towns with many small owners/employers. Adaptive playbooks by community type.

## Product posture

**Build Your Community** and the **Community OS** are design agendas. AI may organize best practices; it does not make policy. No operating town-plan generator is claimed until sourced modules and product design exist.

Research and pilots precede any claim that a real community dashboard is live.
