## Position in the philosophy

Justice is not a side chapter.

In Constitutional Capitalism, **justice is the mechanism that determines whether liberty, ownership, opportunity, and accountability are real—or merely promised.**

A society cannot claim to return power to the people while its justice system can destroy freedom, family, property, employment, housing, voting power, or future because a person is poor, uninformed, overcharged, unable to afford counsel, or trapped in permanent exclusion.

## Foundational principle

> Justice must protect the public, restrain wrongdoing, repair harm where possible, and return people to productive citizenship whenever safely achievable. It must never become a profit center, a political weapon, or a permanent system of social exclusion.

## Central promise

> A just society protects people from harm without surrendering the humanity of those who have caused harm. It holds wrongdoing accountable, repairs what can be repaired, and never converts punishment into permanent power over a person’s life.

## Questions the system must ask

Not only: *What punishment does this person deserve?*

Also: *What happened, who was harmed, what must be repaired, what protects the public, and what pathway returns the offender to responsible participation in society?*

## Five stages

1. **Prevention** — human development and community conditions that reduce predictable harm  
2. **Fair investigation and adjudication** — due process and competent defense  
3. **Proportionate accountability** — response matched to harm, intent, and danger  
4. **Rehabilitation and restoration** — realistic route forward when safe  
5. **Full reentry into citizenship** — no quiet lifetime continuation of punishment by default  

## Justice Test

Every justice policy must answer the ten questions in `justice_framework.json` (`CC-JTEST-01`–`10`), including public protection, due process, proportionality, wealth bias, profit from punishment, victim support, rehabilitation, path to citizenship, official accountability, and long-term power of ordinary people.

## Research before policy

This file records architecture and developing doctrine. It does **not** authorize detailed national justice legislation.

Phase 2 / 2.1 work must:

1. Register primary sources (BJS, DOJ, courts, GAO, state series, peer-reviewed syntheses).  
2. Attach baseline indicators without invented values.  
3. Upgrade claims with supporting and contrary evidence.  
4. Keep legal review at 0% until real legal review occurs.  

Economic justice and criminal justice are treated as inseparable: fees, fines, forfeiture, incarceration costs, and revenue-driven enforcement transfer power away from individuals and must be examined under the no-profit-from-punishment rule.
