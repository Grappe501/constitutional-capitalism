## Position in the philosophy

Another pillar of Constitutional Capitalism: **human capital infrastructure**.

Not only education as schooling — institutions whose purpose is producing capable, healthy, innovative citizens, connected to rural strength, productive capacity, geographic opportunity, ownership, families, and local leadership.

## First principle

> Every child should graduate with a meaningful pathway to productive adulthood. A constitutional republic should intentionally cultivate the talents of every citizen by providing multiple respected pathways—including skilled trades, entrepreneurship, higher education, public service, agriculture, manufacturing, healthcare, technology, and the arts—rather than assuming a single model of success.

## Equal dignity

Additional pathways of equal dignity — not demotions of traditional education. Welding, nursing, software, farming, electrical work, teaching, and engineering all contribute to the same constitutional project of strong communities.

## Human capital infrastructure

States build highways, bridges, broadband, and water systems. They should also deliberately build human infrastructure.

## Pilot posture

Regional academies are **one possible institutional model** to research and pilot — not a single statewide design assumed before testing. The doctrine is portable; Arkansas may demonstrate through pilots.

## Arkansas academy design agenda

Name candidates (undecided): Rural Civic & Innovation Academies; Community Prosperity Academies; Regional Leadership Academies.

Mission: prepare young Arkansans to build prosperous lives in the communities they call home through academic excellence, technical mastery, civic leadership, entrepreneurship, and real-world experience.

Organized by regional economic strengths. Living-learning campuses. Paid apprenticeships. Entrepreneurship equal to trades. Civic leadership. Community advisory partnerships. Flourishing metrics beyond graduation rates.

ASMSA is an institutional reference for residential public academies — not a sourced outcome evaluation in this slice.

Research precedes any statewide academy statute or locked curriculum map.
