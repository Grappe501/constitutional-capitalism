## Position in the philosophy

This is a **second constitutional spine** alongside economic diagnosis and justice.

Constitutional Capitalism cannot return economic power to the people while leaving political power concentrated in legislatures, parties, donors, governors, and courts.

## Foundational principle

> No branch, party, officeholder, donor class, or temporary majority should be able to convert electoral victory into permanent control of the republic.

## Trust doctrine

> Political authority is held in trust, never owned. No officeholder, legislature, party, court, executive, donor, or institution may lawfully convert temporary public authority into permanent control. Power must remain divided, visible, reviewable, locally rooted where possible, and continuously returnable to the people.

## Anti-entrenchment doctrine

> No temporary majority may use public power principally to make itself harder to remove, harder to investigate, or less accountable to the electorate.

## Party principle

> A political party may support a candidate, but it should not own the office, the district, or the voter.

## Distributed government objective

> Strong enough to protect rights and perform necessary public functions, but too divided, transparent, and accountable for any temporary faction to capture permanently.

## Political power hierarchy

Citizen → neighborhood/community → local government → county/regional → state → federal.

Each level receives only power necessary for functions lower levels cannot perform effectively or constitutionally. Constitutional rights remain the floor.

## Legal and institutional context (sourced)

- *Rucho v. Common Cause* (2019): Supreme Court held partisan-gerrymandering claims present political questions beyond federal judicial resolution, leaving much reform to Congress, state constitutions/courts, commissions, and voters (`CC-SRC-014`).
- NCSL materials document variation in state redistricting commissions and legislative term-limit designs (`CC-SRC-015`, `CC-SRC-016`). Term limits alone are not treated as a complete anti-capture solution.
