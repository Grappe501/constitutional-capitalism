# Transition, Testing, and National Transformation Architecture

Status: architectural proposal (post–Phase 1)  
Last updated: 2026-08-04

## Purpose

Capture the implementation, testing, measurement, failure-analysis, and long-horizon transformation layer of Constitutional Capitalism.

This is **not** a claim that outcomes are known. It is a structure for evaluating the philosophy as a living constitutional framework.

## Core distinction

Isolated policies (for example, “raise corporate taxes”) are likely to produce modest or easily avoided effects.

The project’s hypothesis is that **system-level implementation over 10–20 years** is required for meaningful ownership broadening and middle-class renewal — and that this hypothesis must be tested, not asserted as proof.

## Scenario horizon (predictions only)

See `data/project/transition_scenarios.json`.

- Years 1–3: structural groundwork; limited visible wealth-gap movement
- Years 3–7: possible middle-class asset accumulation begins
- Years 7–15: ownership compounding becomes the distinctive mechanism
- Years 10–20: long-term structural and psychological shift if successful

All magnitudes are provisional and require modeling.

## Testing ladder

1. Economic models  
2. State-level pilots  
3. Regional implementation  
4. National adoption  

Each stage must pre-define success metrics and publish failures as well as successes.

## Greatest identified risk

Attempting too much too quickly across interconnected capital markets, retirement systems, trade, governance, and taxation.

Mitigation: principles first; phased rules; prospective application; measurement; amendment.
