# Foundational Doctrine

Last updated: 2026-08-04  
Status: working doctrine — see also `docs/architecture/CIVILIZATIONAL_CORE.md`

## Canonical (locked unless Steve amends)

1. Title and subtitle remain as in `book_identity.json`.  
2. Canonical definition and central belief in `book_identity.json` remain authoritative until formally amended.  
3. Free enterprise creates wealth most effectively under real competition and broad opportunity.  
4. Lasting prosperity requires accountable economic power.  
5. Ownership, work, and local communities are civic as well as economic goods.  
6. Markets need rules that protect liberty rather than entrench domination.  
7. Government must be limited and capable.  
8. The aim is a prosperous, free, and self-governing people — not wealth aggregates alone.

## Proposed civilizational deepening (open decisions)

1. **Sacred sentence:** Power should always flow toward the people, never permanently away from them. (`CC-DEC-034`)  
2. **Organizing principle:** Power should be distributed as close as possible to the people who create and sustain society.  
3. **First principle (with dignity guardrail):** The people are the nation’s greatest source of productive capacity, creativity, innovation, and self-government — never merely resources. (`CC-DEC-039`)  
4. **Subsidiarity:** Acknowledge the shared intuition; extend into ownership and economic benefit. (`CC-DEC-036`)  
5. **Five pillars:** Liberty, Ownership, Accountability, Prosperity, Stewardship. (`CC-DEC-037`)  
6. **Duty of constructive improvement:** Improve the system for the next generation. (`CC-DEC-040`)  
7. **Living medium:** Website as school of thought; book as Introduction. (`CC-DEC-035`)

## Boundaries

Boundaries against capitalism-as-extraction, socialism, Marxism, corporatism, and social democracy: see `WHAT_CONSTITUTIONAL_CAPITALISM_IS_AND_IS_NOT.md`.

Distinguish **normative principles** from **policy proposals**. Inspire without guaranteeing outcomes that cannot honestly be promised.
